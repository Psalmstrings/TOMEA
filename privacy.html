CLIENT ASSURANCE

PRIVACY & CLIENT TERMS

Our commitment to discretion, data privacy, and the highest standards of white-glove customer service.

1. Client Discretion & Confidentiality

At TOMÉA PERFUMES, discretion is an inviolable tenet of luxury. Any personal details provided to our concierge — including your name, contact phone number, delivery address, and bespoke fragrance preferences — are held in the strictest confidence.

We will never sell, rent, monetize, or publicly disclose your contact details or order history to any third-party marketing companies or unauthorized entities. Your data is used exclusively to fulfill your orders, provide dispatch tracking, and keep you informed of private maison releases upon request.

2. WhatsApp Ordering & Payment Security

TOMÉA provides personalized ordering via WhatsApp to grant our patrons direct access to a dedicated fragrance advisor. When you initiate an order:

  • Your order summary, bottle quantity, and recipient details are confirmed directly with our verified concierge representative.
  • Payment is processed through secure, verified banking channels or certified payment links. We do not store or capture debit or credit card details on our public servers.
  • Once payment is verified, your order is scheduled for dispatch with an official electronic receipt.

3. White-Glove Dispatch & Delivery Timelines

Every fragrance is housed in its signature coffret, cushioned with luxury protective wrapping to preserve the structural integrity of the flacon during transit.

  • Lagos Metropolitan Area: 24-hour white-glove courier dispatch directly to your residence, executive office, or private club.
  • Nationwide (Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, etc.): 48 to 72 hours via tracked priority logistics partners.
  • International Dispatch: Available upon bespoke request via DHL Express / FedEx international freight.

4. Authenticity Guarantee

Every flacon of TOMÉA L'Origine, Charme, and Désir is guaranteed 100% genuine and formulated to authentic Extrait de Parfum strength. We use only premium botanical absolutes, fine aroma molecules, and aged resins sourced from certified ethical producers in Grasse, France and across the African continent.

5. Returns & Exchange Protocol

Due to the artisanal, personal-use nature of luxury perfumes and hygiene standards, unsealed bottles cannot be returned once opened.

If your product arrives damaged or you received an incorrect flacon, notify our concierge within 24 hours of receipt with photographic proof. We will coordinate an immediate replacement flacon at no additional courier charge. Unopened presentation boxes with unbroken tamper seals may be exchanged within 48 hours.

“Excellence in service is as essential to TOMÉA as the notes in our flacons.”

6. Inquiries & Legal Compliance

For any legal inquiries, data deletion requests, or questions regarding our client care guidelines, please communicate directly with our compliance director:

Email: concierge@tomeaperfumes.com
Headquarters: TOMÉA PERFUMES, Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria.
