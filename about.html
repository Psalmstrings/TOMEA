The Genesis

Born from an Uncompromising Standard

TOMÉA PERFUMES was born from an uncompromising desire to celebrate true luxury: the quiet assurance that occurs when supreme craftsmanship meets raw individuality. In an era saturated with transient aromas, TOMÉA crafts deliberate, high-potency Extrait de Parfum creations engineered to leave an indelible impression.

Every fragrance in the TOMÉA collection is an intimate conversation between rare global raw materials and the unapologetic confidence of modern African luxury lifestyle. From radiant Italian citrus to sacred ambers, noble oudhs, and velvet musks — our formulations achieve an artful balance between immediate magnetic charm and deep, lingering sillage.

"True luxury does not clamor for attention. It enters with grace, lingers with poise, and is remembered long after you have departed."
— The Founder's Note

Haute Parfumerie

The Three Pillars of TOMÉA

01

Extrait de Parfum Concentration

Our fragrances feature between 25%–35% pure perfume oils — the highest concentration recognized in classical perfumery. This ensures phenomenal longevity of 12+ hours with rich, dimensional evolution on the skin.

02

The Olfactory Architecture

Each blend follows a meticulously tuned pyramid: bright, effervescent top notes that capture the senses, a luxurious multi-faceted heart, and a dense, warm drydown that bonds intimately with your skin chemistry.

03

The Unboxing Ritual

Every bottle is ensconced in a rigid burgundy presentation casket, stamped with warm gold foil and accompanied by the signature TOMÉA luxury ribbon and bespoke carrier bag.
